# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Report 2026

> **Interactive Excel Dashboard | Retail Sales & E-Commerce Analytics**

An interactive **retail sales analytics project built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales performance, order trends, customer demographics, order channels, geographic performance, product categories, and order fulfillment status.

The project transforms raw retail transaction data into an interactive annual dashboard designed to help business stakeholders understand **where sales are coming from, who the customers are, which channels perform best, and how orders are being fulfilled**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to analyze Vrinda Store's annual sales data and answer key business questions such as:

* How do monthly orders compare with sales?
* Which customer gender contributes more to sales?
* Which states generate the highest sales?
* Which sales channels contribute the most orders?
* Which age groups contribute most to orders?
* What percentage of orders are successfully delivered?
* What is the distribution of returned, cancelled, and refunded orders?
* Which product categories contribute to overall sales?
* How does customer behavior vary across age and gender?

The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Excel**, using Pivot Tables, charts, slicers, calculated metrics, and interactive dashboard techniques.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### 👩 Women Drive a Larger Share of Sales

Women contribute approximately **64% of sales**, compared with **36% from men**.

This indicates that women are the dominant customer segment in the analyzed dataset and could be an important target for marketing campaigns and product strategies.

---

### 📦 Order Fulfillment Performance

The majority of orders are successfully delivered:

| Order Status |   Share |
| ------------ | ------: |
| ✅ Delivered  | **92%** |
| ↩️ Returned  |  **3%** |
| ❌ Cancelled  |  **3%** |
| 💰 Refunded  |  **2%** |

A **92% delivery rate** indicates strong fulfillment performance while highlighting opportunities to reduce returns, cancellations, and refunds.

---

### 🛒 Top Sales Channels

The dashboard identifies the major order channels:

| Channel     | Share of Orders |
| ----------- | --------------: |
| 🟠 Amazon   |         **35%** |
| 🔵 Myntra   |         **23%** |
| 🔵 Flipkart |         **22%** |
| 🟢 Ajio     |          **6%** |
| Other       |          **4%** |
| Nalli       |          **5%** |
| Meesho      |          **5%** |

**Amazon is the leading order channel**, followed by Myntra and Flipkart.

This provides useful insight for channel-specific marketing and sales strategies.

---

### 🗺️ Top 5 States by Sales

The dashboard identifies the strongest-performing states:

| Rank | State            |      Sales |
| ---: | ---------------- | ---------: |
|    1 | 🇮🇳 Maharashtra | **₹2.99M** |
|    2 | Karnataka        | **₹2.65M** |
|    3 | Uttar Pradesh    | **₹2.10M** |
|    4 | Telangana        | **₹1.71M** |
|    5 | Tamil Nadu       | **₹1.68M** |

**Maharashtra generates the highest sales** among the top-performing states shown in the dashboard.

---

### 👥 Customer Age & Gender

The dashboard analyzes orders across:

* Teenagers
* Adults
* Seniors

and compares:

* Men
* Women

Women account for a larger proportion of orders across the analyzed age groups, with **adult women representing the strongest segment**.

This can help businesses develop targeted campaigns based on customer demographics.
